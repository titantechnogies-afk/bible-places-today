Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Just One More Lord is a free Bible places, study and reading app. We collect as little as possible and never sell your data.

Without an account

Everything you save — places, verses, highlights, notes, prayers, reading plans, streaks, badges and settings — is stored only on your own device (in your browser's local storage). We cannot see it. Use “Download backup” in My library to keep a copy.

With an account

To use the app you create a free account. We store your email address, your name and country, the profile details you choose to add (church, about me, profile photo, prayer focus) and a copy of your library so it syncs between your devices. This is stored with Supabase, our database provider. Only you can read your library.

Profiles, friends and members list

By default you are listed in “People who have joined” so others can find you and add you as a friend. Other members can see your name, photo, church, country, bio, when you joined, how many friends you have and when you were last online. You can hide yourself from the list and hide your online status at any time. Friends can also see your prayer focus, and your highlights and notes if you choose to share them.

Messages

Messages are private between you and the friend you send them to. We do not read them. If someone reports a message, a copy of that message is kept so the app's admins can review the report. Phone notifications for new messages are sent through your phone's notification service and show the sender's name and the start of the message.

Safety and reports

You can block anyone and report messages or profiles. Reports, the reported content and who sent the report are seen only by the app's admins, who may hide or remove accounts that break our terms of use.

Help and prayer messages

When you send us a private “Need help or prayer?” message, only the app's admins can read it. It is used only to pray for you and reply to you.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in My profile → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your login, profile, photo, synced library, friends, messages, prayer requests and phone-notification registration from our servers. Copies of a message you sent remain only where a report about it is still being reviewed.

Your rights (POPIA)

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, object to how it is used, or ask us to delete it. Most of this you can do yourself in My profile. For anything else, contact us as described below. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Comments, prayer wall and community board

These use giscus and GitHub Discussions. Posting requires a GitHub account and your posts are public on GitHub under GitHub's own privacy policy.

Content from other services

To show photographs, maps, Bible text and articles, your device connects directly to Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, Wikidata, bible-api.com, OpenStreetMap, Esri, OpenTopoMap, Amazon Web Services (terrain), YouTube (teaching videos, only when you press play) and the cdnjs content network. Those services receive your IP address as part of normal web requests.

Location

“Bible places near me” asks for your location only when you press the button. It is used on your device to sort places by distance and is never sent to us.

Visitor statistics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies or track you across sites.

Children

The Bible and teaching in the app are suitable for all ages. You must be at least 13 to create an account, and under-18s need a parent's or guardian's permission.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: send us a private message with Need help or prayer? in the app, or open an issue at github.com/titantechnogies-afk/bible-places-today/issues or use the contact email on the Developer notes page.
