The purpose of Proverbs: to know wisdom. "The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge" (1:7). Wisdom calls out to everyone; Jesus is the wisdom of God (1 Corinthians 1:30).
Seek wisdom like silver and the Lord gives it (2:6). Wisdom protects from the paths that lead to harm.
"Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding" (3:5). God's correction is the love of a Father delighting in his son (3:12).
"Keep your heart with all diligence, for from it flow the springs of life" (4:23). The path of the righteous shines brighter and brighter.
A father warns his son about adultery and celebrates faithful married love. God's design for marriage brings joy.
Warnings about laziness and debt, and seven things God hates. Look at the ant; God's wisdom is practical.
A young man is lured by the adulteress. Wisdom keeps us from paths that destroy; grace empowers us to say no (Titus 2:12).
Wisdom speaks: "I was beside him like a master workman" (8:30). Many see Christ in this picture of wisdom present at creation.
Wisdom's feast and folly's invitation. Jesus invites everyone to his table.
Contrasts between the righteous and the wicked. "The blessing of the Lord makes rich, and he adds no sorrow with it" (10:22).
"Whoever brings blessing will be enriched" (11:25). Generosity flows from a heart that trusts God.
Words have power: "The tongue of the wise brings healing" (12:18).
"Hope deferred makes the heart sick, but a desire fulfilled is a tree of life" (13:12).
"In the fear of the Lord one has strong confidence, and his children will have a refuge" (14:26).
"A gentle answer turns away wrath" (15:1). A cheerful heart is good medicine.
"Commit your work to the Lord, and your plans will be established" (16:3). The heart plans; the Lord directs the steps.
"A friend loves at all times" (17:17). A joyful heart is good medicine (17:22).
"Death and life are in the power of the tongue" (18:21). The name of the Lord is a strong tower.
"Whoever is generous to the poor lends to the Lord" (19:17). The Lord's purpose stands.
"The spirit of man is the lamp of the Lord" (20:27). God guides us from within by his Spirit.
"The king's heart is in the hand of the Lord" (21:1). Victory belongs to the Lord.
"A good name is to be chosen rather than great riches" (22:1). Train up a child in the way he should go.
"As he thinks in his heart, so is he" (23:7). Renewing the mind shapes our lives.
"The righteous falls seven times and rises again" (24:16). In Christ we always rise because of his righteousness.
"If your enemy is hungry, give him bread" (25:21). Paul quotes this: overcome evil with good (Romans 12:20).
Fools, sluggards and gossips. Words can wound or heal; wisdom guards the tongue.
"Iron sharpens iron" (27:17). Faithful friends make each other stronger.
"Whoever conceals his sins will not prosper, but whoever confesses finds mercy" (28:13). In Christ mercy is ours.
"Where there is no vision the people perish" (29:18). The fear of man is a snare; trust in God keeps us safe.
"Every word of God proves true; he is a shield to those who take refuge in him" (30:5). "What is his son's name?" (30:4): Jesus.
The woman of strength: her worth is far above rubies. She fears the Lord and is praised.
@Ecc
"Vanity of vanities, all is vanity". Life "under the sun" without God is empty. Jesus came to give life to the full.
The Preacher tries pleasure, building and wealth, and finds them empty. Only God satisfies the heart.
"For everything there is a season" (3:1). God has put eternity in our hearts (3:11).
"Two are better than one… a threefold cord is not quickly broken" (4:9-12). We need one another and God.
Approach God with reverence, not empty words. Enjoy your work and your food: it is a gift of God (5:19).
Wealth without the ability to enjoy it is empty. Contentment is a gift.
Wisdom in hard times: "Consider the work of God". God has made both the day of prosperity and adversity.
Wisdom brightens the face. No one has power over the day of death; only God does, and Jesus has defeated death.
Life is uncertain, so enjoy life with those you love. "Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with your might" (9:10).
A little folly outweighs wisdom. Wise words are gracious (10:12).
"Cast your bread upon the waters" (11:1). Sow generously; remember your Creator in your youth.
"Remember your Creator in the days of your youth". The conclusion: fear God. In Christ the search ends in relationship with him.

The bride longs for her beloved: "Your love is better than wine" (1:2). A song of love, often seen as a picture of Christ and his church.
"His banner over me was love" (2:4). "Arise, my love, my beautiful one, and come away" (2:10). Jesus calls his bride with love.
The bride searches for her beloved at night and finds him. Those who seek find.
"You are altogether beautiful, my love; there is no flaw in you" (4:7). In Christ we are without spot or blemish (Ephesians 5:27).
The bride hesitates and her beloved withdraws; she seeks him. "This is my beloved and this is my friend" (5:16).
"I am my beloved's and my beloved is mine" (6:3). Belonging and delight.
The beloved delights in his bride. "I am my beloved's, and his desire is for me" (7:10). You are desired by God.
"Love is as strong as death… many waters cannot quench love" (8:6-7). Nothing can quench the love of Christ.
