@Psa
God is known in Zion and breaks the weapons of war. He rises to save all the humble of the earth (76:9).
In a sleepless night of trouble, Asaph remembers the deeds of the Lord. Remembering God's faithfulness lifts us out of despair.
Tell the next generation of God's wonders. Despite Israel's unbelief, "he, being compassionate, forgave their iniquity" (78:38). He remembered they were but flesh.
A lament over Jerusalem's ruin: "Help us, O God of our salvation, for the glory of your name" (79:9).
"Restore us, O God; let your face shine, that we may be saved" (80:3). Jesus is the Son of Man at God's right hand.
"Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it" (81:10). God longs to bless his people.
God judges unjust rulers: "Defend the weak and the fatherless" (82:3). Jesus quoted "you are gods" (John 10:34).
A prayer against enemies conspiring together. God alone is Most High over all the earth.
"Blessed are those whose strength is in you… they go from strength to strength" (84:5-7). The Lord withholds no good thing (84:11).
"Steadfast love and faithfulness meet; righteousness and peace kiss each other" (85:10). At the cross, mercy and justice met.
"You, O Lord, are good and forgiving, abounding in steadfast love to all who call on you" (86:5).
The nations will be born in Zion. In Christ people from every nation are born again into God's family.
The darkest psalm, ending in darkness. Jesus entered our darkness so we would never be alone in it.
God's covenant with David will stand forever, even when his children fail (89:30-34). God's faithfulness does not depend on ours.
"Teach us to number our days" (90:12). Life is short, but God is our dwelling place in every generation.
"He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High will abide in the shadow of the Almighty" (91:1). Protection, long life and salvation for those who trust him.
"It is good to give thanks to the Lord". The righteous flourish like a palm tree and bear fruit in old age.
"The Lord reigns; he is robed in majesty" (93:1). He is mightier than the floods.
"When my anxious thoughts multiply, your comforts delight my soul" (94:19). God is our defence.
"Come, let us sing to the Lord". Hebrews 3-4 applies this psalm: "Today, if you hear his voice", enter his rest by faith.
"Sing to the Lord a new song… declare his glory among the nations" (96:1-3). The gospel is good news for all peoples.
"The Lord reigns, let the earth rejoice". Light is sown for the righteous.
"Sing a new song, for he has done marvellous things; his right hand has worked salvation" (98:1).
"The Lord our God is holy". He answered those who called on him and forgave them (99:8).
"Enter his gates with thanksgiving… for the Lord is good; his steadfast love endures forever" (100:4-5).
David's commitment to walk with integrity. Jesus is the perfect King who rules with integrity.
A prayer of an afflicted person: "You will arise and have pity on Zion". Hebrews 1:10-12 applies its words about the unchanging Creator to Jesus.
"Bless the Lord, O my soul… who forgives all your iniquity, who heals all your diseases" (103:2-3). As far as east from west, he removes our sins.
A hymn to God the Creator who provides for every creature. Send forth your Spirit and the earth is renewed.
God remembers his covenant forever and brings his people out with joy (105:43).
Israel's repeated rebellion, and yet "he saved them for his name's sake" (106:8). Many times he delivered them.
"They cried to the Lord in their trouble, and he delivered them". He sent his word and healed them (107:20).
"With God we shall do valiantly" (108:13). His steadfast love is great above the heavens.
David prays against an accuser. Acts 1:20 applies it to Judas. Jesus is our advocate against the accuser.
"Sit at my right hand until I make your enemies your footstool" (110:1). The most-quoted psalm in the New Testament: Jesus is the eternal King and Priest.
"He sent redemption to his people; he has commanded his covenant forever" (111:9).
"He will not be afraid of bad news; his heart is firm, trusting in the Lord" (112:7).
"He raises the poor from the dust… he gives the barren woman a home" (113:7-9). God lifts the lowly.
At the Exodus the sea fled and the mountains skipped. God turns the rock into a pool of water.
"Not to us, O Lord, but to your name give glory" (115:1). He blesses those who trust him.
"I love the Lord, because he has heard my voice" (116:1). Precious in his sight is the death of his saints.
The shortest psalm: praise the Lord, all nations, for his steadfast love is great (117). Paul quotes it in Romans 15:11.
"The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone" (118:22). Jesus is that stone. "This is the day the Lord has made".
The longest psalm, a love song for God's Word. "Your word is a lamp to my feet" (119:105). Jesus is the living Word.
"In my distress I called to the Lord, and he answered me" (120:1). The first song of ascents.
"My help comes from the Lord, the Maker of heaven and earth" (121:2). He who keeps you will not slumber.
"I was glad when they said to me, Let us go to the house of the Lord" (122:1). Pray for the peace of Jerusalem.
"Our eyes look to the Lord our God, till he has mercy on us" (123:2).
"If it had not been the Lord who was on our side…" (124:1). Our help is in the name of the Lord.
"As the mountains surround Jerusalem, so the Lord surrounds his people" (125:2).
"Those who sow in tears shall reap with joy" (126:5). God restores our fortunes.
"Unless the Lord builds the house, those who build it labour in vain… he gives to his beloved sleep" (127:1-2). Grace, not striving.
"Blessed is everyone who fears the Lord". Family blessing flows from God.
"They have afflicted me… yet they have not prevailed against me" (129:2).
"If you, O Lord, kept a record of sins, who could stand? But with you there is forgiveness" (130:3-4).
"I have calmed and quieted my soul, like a weaned child with its mother" (131:2). Rest in God.
God's oath to David: one of your sons will sit on your throne (132:11). Fulfilled in Jesus.
"How good and pleasant when brothers dwell in unity… there the Lord commanded the blessing" (133).
"Lift up your hands in the sanctuary and bless the Lord" (134:2).
"The Lord has chosen Jacob for himself" (135:4). He is good; idols are nothing.
"His steadfast love endures forever", repeated 26 times. God's love is the reason behind everything he does.
By the rivers of Babylon the exiles weep. A raw lament; in Christ, our enemies are forgiven and we bless those who curse us (Luke 6:28).
"The Lord will fulfil his purpose for me; your steadfast love endures forever" (138:8).
"You have searched me and known me… I am fearfully and wonderfully made" (139:1, 14). Nowhere is beyond God's presence.
"Deliver me, O Lord, from evil men" (140:1). The Lord maintains the cause of the afflicted.
"Let my prayer be counted as incense before you" (141:2).
A prayer from the cave: "You are my refuge, my portion in the land of the living" (142:5).
"Cause me to hear your steadfast love in the morning, for in you I trust" (143:8). Romans 3:20 echoes "no one living is righteous before you".
"Blessed be the Lord, my rock, who trains my hands for war" (144:1).
"The Lord is gracious and merciful, slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love. The Lord is good to all" (145:8-9).
"Do not put your trust in princes" (146:3). The Lord sets prisoners free, opens the eyes of the blind and lifts the bowed down.
"He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds" (147:3). He delights in those who hope in his steadfast love.
All creation is called to praise the Lord: sun, moon, stars, seas, kings and children.
"The Lord takes pleasure in his people; he adorns the humble with salvation" (149:4).
"Let everything that has breath praise the Lord!" (150:6). The Psalms end in pure praise.
