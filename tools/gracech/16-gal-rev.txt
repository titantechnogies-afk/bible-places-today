@Gal
Paul is amazed that the Galatians are turning to a different gospel. There is no other gospel than the grace of Christ (1:6-7).
Paul confronts Peter's hypocrisy: "A person is not justified by works of the law but through faith in Jesus Christ… if righteousness came through the law, Christ died for nothing" (2:16, 21).
"Did you receive the Spirit by works of the law or by hearing with faith?" Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us (3:13). The law was a tutor to bring us to Christ.
You are no longer a slave but a son and heir (4:7). Hagar and Sarah: law and grace cannot be mixed.
"For freedom Christ has set us free; stand firm" (5:1). Falling from grace means trying to be justified by the law (5:4). Walk by the Spirit and his fruit grows: love, joy, peace.
Restore one another gently. "Do not grow weary in doing good". Paul boasts only in the cross. Neither circumcision nor uncircumcision counts, but a new creation (6:15).
@Eph
Blessed with every spiritual blessing; chosen, adopted, accepted in the Beloved, forgiven and sealed with the Spirit (1:3-14). Paul prays we would know the power already in us (1:19).
"By grace you have been saved through faith… not a result of works" (2:8-9). Once far off, now brought near by the blood of Christ; Jesus is our peace.
Gentiles are fellow heirs. Paul prays we would know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge (3:19). God can do far more than we ask or imagine.
Grace was given to each one; Jesus gave gifts to equip the saints. Put on the new self, created in righteousness and holiness. Forgive "as God in Christ forgave you" (4:32).
Be imitators of God as beloved children; walk in love. Be filled with the Spirit. Christ loved the church and gave himself for her (5:25).
Be strong in the Lord and put on the whole armour of God. Our battle is not against flesh and blood. Stand in what Jesus has won.
@Php
"He who began a good work in you will bring it to completion" (1:6). For me to live is Christ, and to die is gain.
Jesus humbled himself to the cross and God exalted him above every name. "Work out" your salvation, "for it is God who works in you" (2:12-13).
Paul counts his law-righteousness as rubbish to be found in Christ, "not having a righteousness of my own… but that which comes through faith" (3:9). Press on.
"Rejoice in the Lord always… do not be anxious… the peace of God will guard your hearts" (4:4-7). "I can do all things through Christ" and "God will supply all your needs" (4:13, 19).
@Col
God has delivered us from the domain of darkness and transferred us into the kingdom of his beloved Son (1:13). Jesus is the image of the invisible God; we are presented holy and blameless.
"In him the whole fullness of deity dwells bodily, and you have been filled in him" (2:9-10). Our debt was nailed to the cross and the powers disarmed. Do not let anyone judge you over rules.
Set your minds on things above, for your life is hidden with Christ in God (3:3). Forgive as the Lord forgave you; put on love.
"Let your speech always be gracious, seasoned with salt" (4:6). Continue steadfastly in prayer with thanksgiving.
@1Th
Paul thanks God for their work of faith, labour of love and steadfast hope. They turned from idols to serve the living God and wait for Jesus, who delivers us from the wrath to come (1:10).
Paul cared for them gently like a nursing mother. God's Word is at work in those who believe.
Paul prays that their love will increase and their hearts be established blameless in holiness.
Jesus will come again and the dead in Christ will rise. "Encourage one another with these words" (4:18).
"God has not destined us for wrath, but to obtain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ" (5:9). Rejoice always, pray without ceasing, give thanks. He who calls you is faithful (5:24).
@2Th
God will give rest to the afflicted. Paul prays God will fulfil every good purpose by his power.
God chose you for salvation through sanctification by the Spirit and belief in the truth. He gives eternal comfort and good hope through grace (2:16).
"The Lord is faithful; he will establish you and guard you against the evil one" (3:3).
@1Ti
"Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the foremost" (1:15). Paul received mercy as an example of God's patience.
God desires all people to be saved; there is one mediator, Christ Jesus, who gave himself as a ransom for all (2:4-6).
Qualities of church leaders. "Great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifested in the flesh" (3:16).
Rules about food and marriage are false; everything God created is good when received with thanksgiving (4:4). Godliness has value for this life and the next.
Care for widows and honour elders. Grace teaches us to care for our families and one another.
"Godliness with contentment is great gain" (6:6). God richly provides us with everything to enjoy (6:17).
@2Ti
"God has not given us a spirit of fear but of power, love and a sound mind" (1:7). He saved us "not because of our works but because of his own purpose and grace" (1:9).
"Be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus" (2:1). "If we are faithless, he remains faithful" (2:13).
Difficult times will come. All Scripture is breathed out by God and equips us for every good work (3:16-17).
"Preach the word". Paul has finished the race; the Lord stood by him and strengthened him.
@Tit
Qualities of elders. To the pure all things are pure.
"The grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to all people, training us to renounce ungodliness" (2:11-12). Grace is the power for godly living.
"He saved us, not because of works done by us in righteousness, but according to his own mercy, by the washing of regeneration and renewal of the Holy Spirit" (3:5).
@Phm
Paul appeals for the runaway slave Onesimus: "If he owes you anything, charge that to my account" (verse 18). This is what Jesus did for us: he took our debt and gave us his standing.
@Heb
God has spoken to us by his Son, the exact imprint of his nature, who made purification for sins and sat down (1:3). He sat down because the work was finished.
Jesus tasted death for everyone and destroyed the devil, who had the power of death (2:9, 14). He is not ashamed to call us brothers.
Jesus is worthy of more glory than Moses. "Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts" (3:15). Unbelief kept Israel from rest.
There remains a rest for the people of God; whoever enters it rests from their own works (4:9-10). Come boldly to the throne of grace (4:16).
Jesus is our High Priest who learned obedience through suffering and became the source of eternal salvation.
Hebrews 6:4-6 warns Jewish believers against returning to the temple sacrifices: if Jesus' sacrifice is rejected there is no other. "We are confident of better things concerning you" (6:9). God's promise is anchored by an oath.
Melchizedek pictures Jesus, a priest forever. He is able to save completely those who come to God through him, since he always lives to intercede (7:25).
The new covenant: "I will remember their sins no more" (8:12). The old covenant is obsolete.
Jesus entered the holy place once for all by his own blood, securing eternal redemption (9:12). His blood cleanses our conscience from dead works.
By one offering he has perfected forever those who are being sanctified (10:14). "Their sins I will remember no more". Draw near with full assurance. (10:26 warns against rejecting his sacrifice.)
The heroes of faith: "Without faith it is impossible to please God" (11:6). They were commended for their faith, not their perfection.
Look to Jesus, the author and finisher of our faith (12:2). God trains us as a loving Father (the Greek word means child-training). You have come to Mount Zion and the blood that speaks better than Abel's.
"I will never leave you nor forsake you" (13:5). Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today and forever (13:8). Grace strengthens the heart.
@Jas
Count it joy in trials. God gives wisdom generously without reproach. He tempts no one; every good and perfect gift comes from the Father of lights (1:17).
Real faith shows itself in action. We are justified before God by faith; living faith produces works people can see (2:18).
The tongue has power. Wisdom from above is pure, peaceable, gentle and full of mercy (3:17).
Submit to God, resist the devil and he will flee (4:7). God gives more grace; he gives grace to the humble.
"The prayer of faith will save the sick, and the Lord will raise him up" (5:15). The prayer of a righteous person has great power.
@1Pe
Born again to a living hope and an inheritance kept in heaven. Redeemed not with silver or gold but with the precious blood of Christ (1:18-19).
You are a chosen people, a royal priesthood (2:9). "By his wounds you were healed" (2:24).
Jesus suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous, to bring us to God (3:18). Always be ready to share the hope within you.
"Love covers a multitude of sins" (4:8). Each has received a gift; use it to serve one another as good stewards of God's grace.
"Cast all your anxieties on him, because he cares for you" (5:7). The God of all grace will restore, confirm and strengthen you.
@2Pe
His divine power has given us everything we need for life and godliness (1:3). We are partakers of the divine nature.
False teachers will arise. God knows how to rescue the godly.
The Lord is patient, not wanting anyone to perish (3:9). "Grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ" (3:18).
@1Jn
God is light and the blood of Jesus cleanses us from all sin (1:7). Confessing means agreeing with God; John wrote against those who denied sin existed.
"If anyone does sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous" (2:1). "Your sins are forgiven for his name's sake" (2:12).
"See what kind of love the Father has given us, that we should be called children of God" (3:1). The Son appeared to destroy the works of the devil (3:8).
God is love. Perfect love casts out fear. "As he is, so are we in this world" (4:17). We love because he first loved us.
"Whoever has the Son has life" (5:12). These things are written so that you may know you have eternal life (5:13).
@2Jn
Walk in truth and love. Jesus truly came in the flesh. Grace, mercy and peace will be with us.
@3Jn
"I pray that you may prosper and be in health, even as your soul prospers" (verse 2). Gaius is commended for his love and hospitality.
@Jud
Some turn grace into a licence for immorality. Keep yourselves in God's love; he is able to keep you from stumbling and present you blameless with great joy (verses 24-25).
@Rev
Jesus loves us and has freed us from our sins by his blood (1:5). He holds the keys of death and Hades. "Fear not".
Letters to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum and Thyatira. Jesus knows his people, encourages them and calls them back to their first love.
Letters to Sardis, Philadelphia and Laodicea. "Those whom I love, I rebuke and train… I stand at the door and knock" (3:19-20). Jesus wants fellowship.
John sees the throne in heaven and worship around it. God is on the throne.
Only the Lamb who was slain is worthy to open the scroll. "By your blood you ransomed people for God from every tribe and language" (5:9).
The seals are opened. History is in the hands of the Lamb.
A great multitude from every nation stands before the throne, washed in the blood of the Lamb. God will wipe away every tear (7:17).
The prayers of the saints rise like incense before God. Your prayers matter in heaven.
Trumpet judgements. Even so, God's heart is for people to turn to him.
John eats the little scroll, sweet in the mouth. God's Word must be taken in.
The two witnesses. "The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ" (11:15).
The accuser is thrown down; "they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb and the word of their testimony" (12:11).
The beasts rise in opposition. God's people persevere through faith.
The Lamb stands on Mount Zion with the redeemed. "Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord" (14:13).
Those who overcame sing the song of Moses and the Lamb: "Just and true are your ways".
The bowls of wrath. God's judgement against evil is real; in Christ we are delivered from wrath.
Babylon, the great prostitute, rides the beast. The Lamb will conquer, for he is Lord of lords.
Babylon falls. God's people are called to come out of her.
The marriage supper of the Lamb. Jesus rides out as Faithful and True, the Word of God, King of kings.
Satan is bound and finally defeated. The dead are judged; those in the book of life are safe.
"Behold, I make all things new" (21:5). God will dwell with his people and wipe away every tear; no more death, mourning or pain.
The river of life and the tree whose leaves heal the nations. "Let the one who is thirsty come; let the one who desires take the water of life without price" (22:17). Grace to the very end.
