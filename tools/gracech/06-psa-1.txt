@Psa
The blessed man delights in God's Word and is like a fruitful tree. Jesus is the truly blessed Man; in him we are planted by streams of living water.
Why do the nations rage? God's King is his Son: "You are my Son; today I have begotten you" (2:7). Fulfilled in Jesus (Acts 13:33). Blessed are all who take refuge in him.
David fleeing Absalom: "You, Lord, are a shield around me, my glory, the lifter of my head" (3:3). God lifts us when we are down.
"In peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone make me dwell in safety" (4:8). Peace comes from trusting God.
A morning prayer: "You surround the righteous with favour as with a shield" (5:12). In Christ we are righteous and surrounded by favour.
A cry for mercy in distress: "The Lord has heard my weeping" (6:8). God hears our tears.
David cries for justice when falsely accused. God is a righteous judge who defends the innocent.
"What is man that you are mindful of him?" (8:4). Hebrews 2 applies this to Jesus, crowned with glory, who brings many sons to glory.
"The Lord is a stronghold for the oppressed" (9:9). He does not forget the cry of the afflicted.
Why do you stand far away, Lord? God sees the oppressed and is the helper of the fatherless (10:14).
"If the foundations are destroyed, what can the righteous do?" The Lord is still on his throne (11:4).
"The words of the Lord are pure words" (12:6). God's promises are refined and trustworthy.
"How long, O Lord?" ends with "I have trusted in your steadfast love" (13:5). Honest lament turns to trust.
"There is none who does good, not even one" (14:3). Paul quotes this in Romans 3: all need grace.
Who may dwell on God's holy hill? Only the blameless. Jesus is the one, and in him we are made blameless (Colossians 1:22).
"You will not abandon my soul to Sheol" (16:10). Peter says this was about Jesus' resurrection (Acts 2:27-31). In God's presence is fullness of joy.
"Keep me as the apple of your eye; hide me in the shadow of your wings" (17:8). You are precious to God.
"The Lord is my rock, my fortress and my deliverer" (18:2). He reached down from on high and drew me out of deep waters.
The heavens declare God's glory, and his Word revives the soul. Creation and Scripture both reveal him.
"Some trust in chariots and some in horses, but we trust in the name of the Lord our God" (20:7).
The king rejoices in God's strength and receives long life. Fulfilled in Jesus, given life forever.
"My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" The psalm of the cross: pierced hands and feet, divided clothes. It ends "He has done it" (22:31): it is finished.
"The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want". Jesus is the Good Shepherd; goodness and mercy pursue you all your days.
"Lift up your heads, O gates, that the King of glory may come in". Jesus, the King of glory, has ascended.
"Remember not the sins of my youth… according to your steadfast love remember me" (25:7). In Christ God remembers our sins no more.
David asks God to examine him. In Christ, God sees us clothed in his righteousness.
"The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear?" (27:1). One thing I ask: to dwell in his presence.
"The Lord is my strength and my shield; my heart trusts in him, and I am helped" (28:7).
The voice of the Lord thunders over the waters. "The Lord blesses his people with peace" (29:11).
"Weeping may last for a night, but joy comes in the morning" (30:5). God turns mourning into dancing.
"Into your hand I commit my spirit" (31:5). Jesus prayed this on the cross. Our times are in God's hands.
"Blessed is the one whose transgression is forgiven… to whom the Lord does not count iniquity" (32:1-2). Paul uses this for righteousness without works (Romans 4:6-8).
"The eye of the Lord is on those who hope in his steadfast love" (33:18). Sing a new song.
"Taste and see that the Lord is good" (34:8). Not one of his bones will be broken (34:20), fulfilled at the cross.
David asks God to fight for him against false accusers. "Let the Lord be magnified, who delights in the welfare of his servant" (35:27).
"Your steadfast love reaches to the heavens… with you is the fountain of life" (36:5, 9).
"Delight yourself in the Lord, and he will give you the desires of your heart" (37:4). Do not fret; trust in the Lord.
David's prayer in sickness and guilt. Under the new covenant Jesus carried our sins and sicknesses; we come with confidence.
"My hope is in you" (39:7). Life is short, but God is our hope.
God lifted me out of the pit and set my feet on a rock (40:2). "Sacrifice you did not desire… a body you prepared" (Hebrews 10:5): Jesus came to do God's will.
"Blessed is he who considers the poor" (41:1). A friend lifted his heel against me (41:9), which Jesus applied to Judas.
"As the deer pants for streams of water, so my soul longs for you" (42:1). Hope in God; I will yet praise him.
"Send out your light and your truth; let them lead me" (43:3). Jesus is the way, the truth and the light.
Israel was not saved by its own sword but by God's favour (44:3). "For your sake we are killed all day long", quoted in Romans 8:36 before "we are more than conquerors".
A royal wedding psalm: "Your throne, O God, is forever" (45:6). Hebrews 1:8 applies it to Jesus, the Bridegroom King.
"God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble" (46:1). Be still and know that he is God.
"God is the King of all the earth" (47:7). Jesus has ascended with a shout.
Zion, the city of the great King. "This God is our God forever; he will guide us" (48:14).
Riches cannot redeem a soul, "but God will redeem my soul" (49:15). Jesus paid the ransom.
God does not need sacrifices: "Call on me in the day of trouble; I will deliver you" (50:15). He wants relationship.
David's confession after Bathsheba: "Create in me a clean heart". In Christ, God has given us a new heart and a Spirit who never leaves (Hebrews 13:5).
"I am like a green olive tree in the house of God; I trust in the steadfast love of God forever" (52:8).
"There is none who does good". Paul quotes this (Romans 3). All need the grace of God.
"God is my helper; the Lord upholds my life" (54:4).
"Cast your burden on the Lord, and he will sustain you" (55:22). Betrayal by a friend, a picture of Jesus' betrayal.
"When I am afraid, I put my trust in you" (56:3). God keeps every tear in his bottle.
"I cry to God who fulfils his purpose for me" (57:2). His love reaches the heavens.
God will judge the unjust. "Surely there is a God who judges on earth" (58:11).
"You have been my fortress and refuge in the day of my distress" (59:16).
"With God we shall do valiantly" (60:12). Victory comes from God, not human help.
"Lead me to the rock that is higher than I" (61:2). Jesus is our rock.
"My soul waits in silence for God alone; from him comes my salvation" (62:1).
"Your steadfast love is better than life" (63:3). David seeks God in the wilderness.
God turns the enemy's arrows back on them. The righteous rejoice in the Lord.
"Blessed is the one you choose and bring near" (65:4). God crowns the year with his goodness.
"Come and see what God has done" (66:5). He has not removed his steadfast love from me.
"May God be gracious to us and bless us and make his face shine on us, that your way may be known on earth" (67:1-2).
"Father of the fatherless" (68:5). He ascended on high and gave gifts to men (Ephesians 4:8).
"Zeal for your house has consumed me… they gave me vinegar to drink" (69:9, 21). Fulfilled in Jesus' life and on the cross.
"Make haste to help me, O God!" God is our help and deliverer.
"Even to old age and grey hairs, O God, do not forsake me" (71:18). God is faithful through every season of life.
A prayer for the king whose reign brings justice to the poor and blessing to all nations. Fulfilled in Jesus' kingdom.
Asaph envies the wicked until he enters God's sanctuary. "Whom have I in heaven but you?" (73:25). God is our portion forever.
A lament over the destroyed sanctuary. "God my King is from of old, working salvation in the midst of the earth" (74:12).
God is the judge; he lifts up and brings down. Promotion comes from the Lord.
