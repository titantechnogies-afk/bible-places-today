Angels announce the births of John and Jesus. Mary believes: "Let it be to me according to your word" (1:38). "With God nothing will be impossible" (1:37).
Jesus is born in a manger: "Good news of great joy for all people… a Saviour, who is Christ the Lord" (2:10-11). "Peace on earth, goodwill toward men".
John preaches; at Jesus' baptism the Father says "You are my beloved Son; with you I am well pleased" (3:22). The genealogy reaches back to Adam: Jesus is for all humanity.
Jesus reads Isaiah 61 in Nazareth: "The Spirit of the Lord is upon me… to proclaim liberty to the captives" (4:18). He heals all who come to him.
The miraculous catch of fish: Peter says "Depart from me, I am a sinful man", and Jesus says "Do not be afraid" (5:10). He heals a leper and forgives a paralysed man.
"Love your enemies… be merciful, even as your Father is merciful" (6:35-36). "Give, and it will be given to you" (6:38).
Jesus raises a widow's son and forgives the sinful woman who washes his feet: "Her sins, which are many, are forgiven, for she loved much" (7:47).
The sower parable; Jesus calms the storm, frees the demon-possessed man, heals the woman who touched him and raises Jairus' daughter.
Jesus sends the twelve to heal and preach and feeds the 5,000. He rebukes his disciples for wanting fire from heaven: "The Son of Man came not to destroy lives but to save them" (9:55-56).
The seventy return rejoicing; Jesus says rejoice that your names are written in heaven. The Good Samaritan shows God's mercy. Mary chooses the one thing needed: sitting at Jesus' feet.
Jesus teaches the Lord's Prayer: "How much more will the heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him" (11:13).
Do not be anxious: "It is your Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom" (12:32). You are worth more than many sparrows.
Jesus heals a woman bent over for 18 years, "whom Satan has bound" (13:16). Sickness is bondage that Jesus loosens.
The great banquet: bring in the poor, crippled, blind and lame. God's house will be filled with those who know they need grace.
The lost sheep, the lost coin and the prodigal son. The father runs to his son, embraces him and restores him before he finishes his speech. This is the Father's heart.
The shrewd manager and the rich man and Lazarus. Use earthly things for eternal good.
Ten lepers are healed; one returns to thank Jesus. "The kingdom of God is within you" (17:21).
The persistent widow and the Pharisee and the tax collector: the one who simply asked for mercy went home justified (18:14). Jesus welcomes children.
Zacchaeus climbs a tree and Jesus invites himself to his house: "The Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost" (19:10). Grace changed Zacchaeus' heart.
Jesus answers his critics wisely. The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone.
The widow's offering. Jesus foretells the fall of Jerusalem: "By your endurance you will gain your lives". He is coming back.
The Last Supper: "This cup is the new covenant in my blood" (22:20). Jesus prays for Peter that his faith will not fail, and restores the servant's ear.
Jesus is crucified: "Father, forgive them" (23:34). He promises the thief paradise today, a man who had no time for good works. Salvation is by grace.
The empty tomb. On the road to Emmaus Jesus shows from all the Scriptures how they point to him (24:27). "Repentance and forgiveness of sins will be proclaimed in his name to all nations" (24:47).
@Jhn
"In the beginning was the Word… and the Word became flesh" (1:1, 14). "The law came through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ" (1:17). Behold the Lamb of God.
Jesus turns water into wine at a wedding. Moses' first miracle turned water to blood (death); Jesus' first turned water into wine (life and joy).
"You must be born again" (3:3). "God so loved the world that he gave his only Son… God did not send his Son to condemn the world, but to save it" (3:16-17).
Jesus offers living water to a Samaritan woman with a broken past. He did not condemn her; he revealed himself to her.
Jesus heals a man at the pool of Bethesda who had waited 38 years. "Whoever hears my word and believes… does not come into judgement, but has passed from death to life" (5:24).
Jesus feeds 5,000: "I am the bread of life". "This is the work of God, that you believe in him whom he has sent" (6:29). "Whoever comes to me I will never cast out" (6:37).
"If anyone thirsts, let him come to me and drink… rivers of living water will flow from his heart" (7:37-38), speaking of the Spirit.
The woman caught in adultery: "Neither do I condemn you; go and sin no more" (8:11). "If the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed" (8:36).
Jesus heals a man born blind. Not because of sin, but "that the works of God might be displayed in him" (9:3). Sickness is not punishment.
"I am the good shepherd… The thief comes to steal, kill and destroy; I came that they may have life abundantly" (10:10-11). No one can snatch them from my hand.
Jesus weeps and raises Lazarus: "I am the resurrection and the life" (11:25).
Mary anoints Jesus and he enters Jerusalem. "When I am lifted up, I will draw all people to myself" (12:32). He came not to judge but to save.
Jesus washes his disciples' feet, including Judas'. "Love one another as I have loved you" (13:34).
"I am the way, the truth and the life". "Whoever has seen me has seen the Father" (14:9). The Spirit will be with you forever. "Peace I leave with you".
"I am the vine; you are the branches… abide in me". The vinedresser lifts the branches; "you are already clean because of the word I have spoken" (15:3). "I have called you friends".
The Spirit will convict the world of sin "because they do not believe in me", and of righteousness (16:9-10). Ask in my name, that your joy may be full.
Jesus prays for his disciples and for us, that the Father loves us as he loves Jesus (17:23). Eternal life is knowing God.
Jesus is arrested and tried; Peter denies him. Jesus willingly lays down his life.
Jesus is crucified and cries "It is finished" (19:30): paid in full. Nothing can be added to what he did.
Mary Magdalene meets the risen Jesus. "Peace be with you", and he breathes on them: "Receive the Holy Spirit" (20:22). These things are written that you may believe and have life.
Breakfast on the beach: Jesus restores Peter three times with "Do you love me?" No condemnation, only restoration and a new commission.
