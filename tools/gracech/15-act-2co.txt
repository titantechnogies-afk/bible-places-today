@Act
Jesus promises power when the Holy Spirit comes, and ascends. "You will be my witnesses" (1:8). The church waits in prayer.
Pentecost: the Spirit is poured out and Peter preaches. "Repent and be baptised… for the forgiveness of sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit" (2:38). 3,000 are saved.
Peter and John heal a lame man: "What I have I give you: in the name of Jesus Christ, walk" (3:6). Believers carry Jesus' power.
"There is salvation in no one else" (4:12). The believers pray for boldness and the place is shaken.
Ananias and Sapphira lie to the Holy Spirit. Many are healed even by Peter's shadow; the apostles rejoice to suffer for Jesus.
Deacons are chosen so the apostles can focus on prayer and the Word. Stephen, full of grace and power, does great wonders.
Stephen recounts Israel's history and is stoned, praying "Lord, do not hold this sin against them" (7:60), just as Jesus prayed.
Philip preaches in Samaria with miracles and great joy, then leads the Ethiopian eunuch to Jesus. The gospel reaches outsiders.
Saul meets the risen Jesus on the road to Damascus. The persecutor becomes the apostle of grace: no one is beyond God's reach.
Peter preaches to Cornelius' household: "Everyone who believes in him receives forgiveness of sins" (10:43), and the Spirit falls before they do anything but believe. Jesus healed "all who were oppressed by the devil" (10:38).
Peter explains that God gave the Gentiles the same gift. The church in Antioch: "the grace of God" is seen and Barnabas rejoices (11:23).
An angel frees Peter from prison while the church prays. God answers prayer, sometimes before we finish praying.
Paul and Barnabas are sent out. "Through this man forgiveness of sins is proclaimed… by him everyone who believes is justified from everything you could not be justified from by the law of Moses" (13:38-39).
Paul heals a man lame from birth at Lystra. They strengthened the disciples, saying that through many tribulations we enter the kingdom.
The Jerusalem council decides Gentiles are not under the law of Moses: "We believe we are saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus, just as they are" (15:11).
Paul and Silas sing in prison at midnight; the jailer asks "What must I do to be saved?" "Believe in the Lord Jesus" (16:31).
Paul in Thessalonica, Berea and Athens. The Bereans examined the Scriptures daily (17:11). God "is not far from each one of us".
Paul in Corinth: "Do not be afraid… for I am with you" (18:9-10).
In Ephesus, people receive the Holy Spirit and extraordinary miracles happen through Paul (19:11-12). The Word grew mightily.
Paul raises Eutychus and says farewell to the Ephesian elders, commending them "to the word of his grace" (20:32).
Paul goes to Jerusalem despite warnings. "I am ready to die for the name of the Lord Jesus".
Paul shares his testimony of meeting Jesus. Our story of grace is a powerful witness.
The Lord stands by Paul at night: "Take courage" (23:11). God protects Paul from a murder plot.
Paul before Felix speaks of righteousness and faith in Christ. God's purposes continue in delays.
Paul appeals to Caesar. God uses even legal systems to fulfil his plans.
Paul before Agrippa: Jesus sent him "to open their eyes… that they may receive forgiveness of sins and an inheritance" (26:18).
The storm and shipwreck: God promises no life will be lost, and all reach land safely. God's word holds through the storm.
On Malta Paul shakes off a viper and heals many, then preaches the kingdom in Rome "with all boldness, unhindered" (28:31).
@Rom
The gospel is the power of God for salvation; in it the righteousness of God is revealed from faith to faith (1:16-17). Humanity without God spirals into darkness.
God's kindness leads to repentance (2:4). Outward religion without an inward change does not make anyone right with God.
All have sinned and fall short, and all are justified freely by his grace through the redemption in Christ Jesus (3:23-24). Righteousness comes apart from the law, through faith.
Abraham was counted righteous by believing God. "To the one who does not work but believes in him who justifies the ungodly, his faith is counted as righteousness" (4:5).
Justified by faith, we have peace with God (5:1). God proved his love while we were sinners. Where sin abounded, grace abounded much more (5:20).
Should we sin so grace may abound? No! We died to sin with Christ. Sin shall not be master over you, for you are not under law but under grace (6:14).
The struggle of a person trying to overcome sin by the law. "Who will deliver me?… Thanks be to God through Jesus Christ our Lord" (7:24-25).
"There is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus" (8:1). The Spirit of adoption cries "Abba, Father". Nothing can separate us from God's love.
God's choice is by grace, "not because of works but because of him who calls" (9:11). Gentiles who did not pursue righteousness obtained it by faith.
Christ is the end of the law for righteousness to everyone who believes (10:4). "If you confess with your mouth… and believe in your heart… you will be saved" (10:9). Faith comes by hearing (10:17).
"If it is by grace, it is no longer on the basis of works; otherwise grace would no longer be grace" (11:6). God's gifts and calling are irrevocable.
"Be transformed by the renewing of your mind" (12:2). Every believer has gifts by grace. Overcome evil with good.
Love is the fulfilling of the law (13:10). Put on the Lord Jesus Christ.
Do not judge one another over food and days. "The kingdom of God is righteousness, peace and joy in the Holy Spirit" (14:17).
"May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing" (15:13). Accept one another as Christ accepted you.
Paul greets many fellow workers, women and men. "The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet" (16:20).
@1Co
Paul calls a messy church "sanctified in Christ Jesus, called saints" (1:2). Christ is our wisdom, righteousness, sanctification and redemption (1:30).
"We have the mind of Christ" (2:16). The Spirit reveals what God has prepared for those who love him.
Believers are God's temple and the Spirit dwells in you (3:16). Our works will be tested, but salvation is secure through Christ the foundation.
"What do you have that you did not receive?" (4:7). Everything is a gift; boasting is excluded.
A case of immorality addressed so the person might be saved. Christ our Passover has been sacrificed (5:7).
"Such were some of you, but you were washed, sanctified and justified" (6:11). You were bought with a price; your body is the temple of the Holy Spirit.
Marriage and singleness. Each has their own gift from God.
Food offered to idols: knowledge puffs up, love builds up. Our freedom is used in love for others.
Paul becomes all things to all people to win some. He runs to receive an imperishable crown.
God is faithful: he will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear, and provides a way of escape (10:13). The Rock was Christ.
The Lord's Supper: "This cup is the new covenant in my blood". "Discerning the body" includes seeing what his broken body bought: wholeness (11:29-30).
Spiritual gifts are given by the same Spirit for the good of all. Every member of the body matters.
Love is patient and kind, never fails. Without love, gifts are nothing. God's love is the foundation.
Pursue love and desire spiritual gifts, especially prophecy, which builds up, encourages and comforts (14:3).
Christ died for our sins and rose again. "Thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ" (15:57). Death has lost its sting.
A collection for the saints. "Let all that you do be done in love" (16:14).
@2Co
"The God of all comfort comforts us so we can comfort others" (1:3-4). All God's promises are "Yes" in Christ (1:20).
Paul urges the church to forgive and comfort a repentant brother. We are the fragrance of Christ.
The new covenant is of the Spirit, not the letter; the law was the "ministry of death" and "condemnation", but the new covenant has far greater glory (3:6-11). With unveiled faces we are transformed.
"We have this treasure in jars of clay" (4:7). Our light affliction is producing an eternal weight of glory.
"If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation" (5:17). God was reconciling the world, not counting their sins; he made Jesus sin for us so we become the righteousness of God (5:19-21).
"Now is the day of salvation" (6:2). We are the temple of the living God.
Godly sorrow leads to repentance without regret. Paul rejoices at the church's comfort.
Jesus became poor so that through his poverty you might become rich (8:9). Giving is a grace.
"God loves a cheerful giver… God is able to make all grace abound to you" (9:7-8). Giving is from the heart, not under compulsion.
Our weapons are not carnal but mighty through God; take every thought captive to Christ (10:4-5).
Paul warns against "another Jesus" and a different gospel. He lists his sufferings for the gospel.
The thorn was "a messenger of Satan" (12:7). God said "My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness" (12:9).
"Examine yourselves to see whether you are in the faith… Jesus Christ is in you" (13:5). The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you all.
