**Full description:**

Walk through the world of the Bible.

Just One More Lord brings the places of the Bible to life — where they are, what they look like today, what archaeologists have found there, and every passage that mentions them. Then read, study, pray and grow, all in one free app.

EXPLORE THE BIBLE LANDS
• 100 Bible places with photographs, maps, timelines and archaeology
• Journeys of Abraham, Moses, Jesus and Paul — with 3D fly-overs over the real terrain
• Kingdom maps from David and Solomon to the time of Jesus
• Bible timeline and family tree from Adam to Jesus
• "How far was it?" — distances and walking days
• Bible places near me for travellers

READ AND STUDY
• Full Bible reader with five translations (WEB, KJV, ASV, BBE, YLT)
• Listen to any chapter read aloud
• Tap any word for its Hebrew or Greek meaning (Strong's Concordance)
• 250,000 cross-references
• Ask questions like "Where was Paul shipwrecked?"

GROW EVERY DAY
• Verse of the day and devotionals from the Bible lands
• Reading plans, including the whole Bible in a year
• Scripture memory with daily practice
• Prayer journal and a community prayer wall
• Streaks and badges

FOR FAMILIES AND CHURCHES
• Kids' corner with stories, a quiz and colouring pages
• Group study pages to print or share
• Presentation mode for sermons and classes
• Quizzes to play with friends

Free. No adverts. Works offline. Your notes stay on your phone unless you choose to create an account.
